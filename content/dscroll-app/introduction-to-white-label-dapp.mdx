# Introduction to the White-Label dApp

The **DScroll App** is a fully open-source, next-generation Web3 naming registrar template. It empowers TLD owners, community builders, and decentralized organizations to launch their own customized identity minting portals in minutes.

---

## 💎 Free & Open-Source Sovereignty

Historically, setting up a domain registrar required custom smart contract audits, front-end blockchain integration libraries, and platform listing partnerships. DScroll shifts this control back to the creator:

* **Zero Fees from DScroll**: DScroll charges **absolutely nothing** for deploying or using this portal template. It is fully open-source, allowing you to run your own business model with 100% of the revenue flowing to your own wallet.
* **Completely Self-Custodied**: You host the code on your own repository, deploy it under your own domain name, and configure your own environment parameters.
* **Audited Security Foundation**: The underlying blockchain integration is highly optimized for fast, secure wallet interactions, allowing your users to register names with complete trust.

## ⚙️ Key Operator Features

When you clone and deploy your own instance of the DScroll App, you gain access to two major built-in administrative and consumer dashboards:

* **My Names Dashboard**: A comprehensive personal center where users set their **Primary Identity**, update their decentralized **Off-Chain Profile Records**, and **Sync Ownership** states.
* **Airdrop Dashboard**: An advanced operator campaign manager where TLD owners **Create Airdrops** to distribute free domains to community members and track them via **My Campaigns**.
