# Tracking Airdrop Campaigns (My Campaigns)

Running successful brand campaigns requires tracking and analytics. Under the **Airdrop** primary menu, the **My Campaigns** tab gives TLD operators complete visibility into historical and active identity distributions.

---

## 📈 The Campaign Tracking Dashboard

When you open **My Campaigns**, the DScroll App scans the blockchain logs for your specific TLD registrar contract. It then compiles a detailed, clean list of all administrative domain-mints.

This gives you a real-time, tamper-proof registry of all distributed promotional or VIP identities.

---

## 📊 Available Campaign Analytics

The tracking panel lists all historical airdrop logs with key analytics:

| Metric / Log | Description | Example Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Identities Distributed** | Total number of sub-names minted via campaigns | `142 names` |
| **Active Recipients** | Count of unique wallets that received an airdrop | `128 unique addresses` |
| **Mint Timestamp** | Exact block time when the domain was generated | `2026-05-24 16:32 UTC` |
| **Recipient Address** | Mapped wallet receiving the identity NFT | `0x71C234...` |
| **Transaction Hash** | Direct link to the block explorer | [BaseScan Tx](https://basescan.org) |

---

## 🔍 How to Monitor Your Airdrops

To review your campaign history:

1. Connect your operator wallet to the DScroll App.
2. Select the **Airdrop** menu.
3. Click the **My Campaigns** tab.
4. The dashboard will automatically query on-chain events and render your list.
5. Use the filter search bar to lookup specific sub-names (e.g. searching for `ceo` to see who received the `ceo@brand` identity) or specific wallet addresses.
