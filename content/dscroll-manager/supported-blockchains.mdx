# Supported Blockchain Network

DScroll is built from the ground up for low transaction costs, high throughput, and robust decentralization. To provide the best possible developer and user experience, DScroll is deployed exclusively on the **Base** blockchain.

---

## 🌐 Supported Network

### 🔵 Base Blockchain (Active / Production)
The flagship deployment network for DScroll is **Base**, the Ethereum Layer 2 incubated by Coinbase.
* **Why Base?**: Base offers extremely low transaction fees (often less than a cent), fast block confirmation times, and secure settlement on the Ethereum L1.
* **Status**: Fully active. You can mint, manage, and register sub-identities on Base Mainnet today.

## 💎 The TLD NFT & Marketplace Identity

A Sovereign TLD is fundamentally represented on-chain as a unique, self-custodied NFT. It is important to distinguish how this asset is referenced depending on the context:

* **In the Marketplace (e.g., OpenSea, Blur)**: The NFTs are listed, traded, and recognized under the collection name **ODude**.
* **In the DScroll Manager**: Within the administrative dashboard (`manager.dscroll.com`), these assets are referred to and managed as **TLDs**.

> [!TIP]
> Always verify that your wallet is connected to the **Base** network when accessing `manager.dscroll.com` to manage your existing domains or perform administrative edits.
