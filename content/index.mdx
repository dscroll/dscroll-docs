# 🌐 Welcome to the DScroll Ecosystem

Welcome to the comprehensive documentation hub for **DScroll**, a decentralized, sovereign Web3 digital identity platform. DScroll empowers creators, brands, communities, and DAOs to issue their own white-label Top-Level Domains (TLDs) and custom identity names without writing any custom blockchain smart contracts.

Under the DScroll architecture:
*   **Sovereign TLDs**: Minted as unique, self-custodied NFTs on-chain, eliminating recurring annual domain rent.
*   **ODude Protocol**: The underlying protocol identity and utility engine powering the ecosystem.
*   **Formatting Paradigm**: Employs clean, modern `@` formatting (such as `username@brand`) rather than legacy dot-notation.

---

## 🗺️ Core Application Platforms

The DScroll software suite consists of two distinct user-facing applications designed to coordinate sovereign identity creation and management:

### 🛠️ DScroll Manager
The administrative command center for TLD owners to configure, protect, and monetize their custom registries on-chain.
*   Set identity registration prices.
*   Configure custom payment ERC20 tokens or default to native gas.
*   Adjust registration fees and currency dynamically.
*   Host built-in registry name airdrop campaigns.
*   👉 **[Explore DScroll Manager Documentation](/docs/dscroll-manager)**

### 🌐 DScroll App
The public consumer lookup portal and open-source white-label foundation.
*   Check username and TLD availability in real-time.
*   Register secure, lifetime sub-name identities directly to your cryptographic wallet.
*   Deploy branded registrar portals using our ready-to-run GitHub template.
*   👉 **[Explore DScroll App Documentation](/docs/dscroll-app)**

## 💎 Key Ecosystem Pillars

### 🏷️ Names & Sovereignty
Your identity begins with a unique handle (e.g., `user@brand`). Once registered, it is a secure digital asset owned permanently in your wallet with zero annual renewal fees.

### 🪙 Token Utility & Payments
Registry operators can tie their custom ERC20 utility token directly to registrations, creating organic demand and trading volume inside their project's micro-economy.

### 🛡️ Secure Off-Chain Profiles
DScroll supports gas-free off-chain profile updates (like descriptions, avatars, and contact links) authenticated via cryptographic signature verification.
